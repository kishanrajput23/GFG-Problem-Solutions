## [Insert at Middle of Linked List](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/insert-in-middle-of-linked-list/1)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** Linked List  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given the head of a Singly Linked List and a value x. Insert the key in the middle of the linked list.</span></p>
