## [Maximum Volume of Cuboid](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/magical-box5306/1)

**Difficulty:** Easy  
**Topics:** Mathematics, Geometric  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given two integers <strong>p</strong>&nbsp;and <strong>a</strong>, where,</span></p>
<ul>
<li><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>p</strong>&nbsp;</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">represents</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">&nbsp;the perimeter </span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>a</strong> represents the surface area of a cuboid,&nbsp;</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Find the maximum possible volume of the cuboid and return it</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">&nbsp;rounded to 2 decimal places.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Note:</strong> It is guaranteed that a valid cuboid always exists for the given values.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>p = 22, a = 15
<strong>Output: </strong>3.02
<strong>Explanation: </strong>The maximum attainable volume of the cuboid is 3.02</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>p = 20, a = 5<strong><br></strong><strong>Output: </strong>0.33
<strong>Explanation: </strong>The maximum attainable volume of the cuboid is 0.33</span></pre>

**Expected Complexities:**

Time Complexity: O(1)  
Auxiliary Space: O(1)
