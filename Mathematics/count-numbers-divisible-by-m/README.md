## [Divisible by M in a Range](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/count-numbers-divisible-by-m1524/1)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** Mathematics  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given two numbers a and b which define a range, where a ≤&nbsp;b. Find the count of total numbers in the given range [a … b] divisible by m<strong>.</strong><br><br><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input</strong>: a = 6, b = 15, m = 3
<strong>Output:</strong>&nbsp;4&nbsp;
<strong>Explanation</strong>: 6, 9, 12, 15 are divisible by 3. Hence answer is 4.</span>
</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>a = 25, b = 100, m = 30
<strong>Output:&nbsp;</strong>3
<strong>Explanation</strong>: 30, 60, 90 are divisible by 30. Hence answer is 3.</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1 ≤ a, b, m ≤ 10<sup>6</sup></span></p>
