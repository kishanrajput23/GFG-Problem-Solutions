## [Sum of Odd Squares](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/power-of-pow-odd-numbers1103/1)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** Mathematics, Numbers  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a single integer <strong>n</strong>, your task is to find the sum of the square of the first <strong>n </strong>odd natural Numbers.<br></span><br><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>2
<strong>Output: </strong>10
<strong>Explanation: </strong>1<sup>2 + </sup>3<sup>2</sup><sup> </sup>= 10</span><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>&nbsp;</strong></span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>3
<strong>Output: </strong>35
<strong>Explanation: </strong>1<sup>2</sup> + 3<sup>2</sup> + 5<sup>2</sup> = 35</span>&nbsp;&nbsp;</pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1 ≤ n ≤ 800</span></p>

**Expected Complexities:**

Time Complexity: O(1)  
Auxiliary Space: O(1)
