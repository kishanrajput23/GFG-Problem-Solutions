## [Power of Pow](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/power-of-pow-even-number5440/1)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** Mathematics, Numbers  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given a single integer <strong>n</strong>, your task is to find the sum of the square of the first <strong>n even natural Numbers</strong>.</span>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n =<strong> </strong>2
<strong>Output: </strong>20
<strong>Explanation: </strong>2<sup>2 + </sup>4<sup>2 </sup>= 20</span>
</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 3
<strong>Outptut: </strong>56
<strong>Explanation: </strong>2<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;+ 4<sup>2</sup>&nbsp;+ 6<sup>2</sup> = 56</span>&nbsp;</pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong><br>1 ≤ n ≤ 100</span></p>

**Expected Complexities:**

Time Complexity: O(1)  
Auxiliary Space: O(1)
