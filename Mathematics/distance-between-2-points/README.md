## [Distance between 2 points](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/distance-between-2-points3200/1)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** Mathematics, Geometric  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given coordinates of <strong>2</strong> points as (</span><span style="font-size: 18px; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">x1, y1, x2 and y2</span><span style="font-size: 18px; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">) on a cartesian plane, find the distance between them and </span><strong style="font-size: 18px; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">round </strong><span style="font-size: 18px; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">the result to the nearest integer.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>0 0 2 -2
<strong>Output: </strong>3
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Distance between (0, 0) and (2, -2) is 3.</span>
</pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>-20 23 -15 68
<strong>Output: </strong>45
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Distance between (-20, 23) and (-15, 68) is 45.</span>
</pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constraints:</strong></span><br><span style="font-size: 18px;">-1000 &lt;= x1, y1, x2, y2 &lt;= 1000</span></p>

**Expected Complexities:**

Time Complexity: O(1)  
Auxiliary Space: O(1)
