## [Sum of GP](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/sum-of-gp2120/1)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** Mathematics  

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given <strong>n</strong>, <strong>a</strong> and <strong>r</strong> as the number of terms, first term and common ratio respectively of a Geometric Progression series. Find the sum of the series up to <strong>nth</strong> term.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">The Geometric Progression series looks like :- a, ar, ar<sup>2</sup>, ar<sup>3</sup>, ar<sup>4</sup>, .....&nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;</span>&nbsp;</p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n<strong> = </strong>3, a = 3, r = 2
<strong>Output: </strong>21
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Series upto 3rd term is 3 6 12, so sum will be 21.
</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>n<strong> = </strong>3, a = 1, r = 2
<strong>Output: </strong>7
<strong>Explanation</strong><strong>: </strong>Series upto 3rd term is 1 2 4, so sum will be 7.
</span></pre>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Constra</strong></span><strong style="font-size: 18px;">i</strong><strong style="font-size: 18px;">nts:</strong></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;">1&nbsp;<span style="color: #273239; font-family: Nunito; font-size: 17px; background-color: #ffffff;">≤</span>&nbsp;n, a, r&nbsp;<span style="color: #273239; font-family: Nunito; font-size: 17px; background-color: #ffffff;">≤</span> 9</span></p>

**Expected Complexities:**

Time Complexity: O(log n)  
Auxiliary Space: O(1)
