## [Arithmetic Operators](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/arithmetic-operators/1?page=1)

**Difficulty:** Basic  
**Topics:** python-operators, Java-Operators, Operators, cpp-operator  

**Expected Complexities:**

Time Complexity: O(1)
Auxiliary Space: O(1)

**Problem Description:**

<p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Given two integer variables x and y, perform the following operations:</span></p>
<ul>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>p:</strong> Addition of x and y</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>q:</strong> Subtraction of y from x</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>r:</strong> Multiplication of x and y</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>s:</strong> Floating-point division of x by y</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>t:</strong> Integer division of x by y</span></li>
<li><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>u:</strong> Modulo (remainder when x is divided by y)</span></li>
</ul>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input</strong></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>:</strong></span> <span style="font-size: 18px;">x = 1, y = 2
<strong>Output:</strong> 3 -1 2 0.500 0 1</span><span style="font-size: 18px;">
<strong>Explanation</strong>: The given operations are performed:<br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Addition of x and y</span><span style="font-size: 18px;"> = 3<br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Subtraction of y from x</span><span style="font-size: 18px;"> = -1<br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Multiplication of x and y</span><span style="font-size: 18px;"> = 2<br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Floating-point division of x by y</span><span style="font-size: 18px;"> = 0.500<br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Integer division of x by y</span><span style="font-size: 18px;"> = 0<br>Modulo of x and y = 1<br>Hence, the output is 3 -1 2 0.500 0 1.
</span></pre>
